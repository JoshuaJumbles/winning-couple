# 0004 — Business entity and Apple enrollment type

- **Status:** Open — decision needed before the App Store listing
- **Date raised:** 2026-09-25

## Context

The App Store shows a **seller name** on every listing, and it comes from the
Apple Developer Program enrollment type:

- **Individual** — the seller name is the developer's legal name.
- **Organization** — the seller name is the legal entity, and enrollment
  requires a D-U-N-S number and verification.

Changing type after the fact is possible but is a re-verification, not a
setting. The current enrollment is active; type needs confirming.

Relevant history: a New York sole proprietorship, *Jelly Bean Sciences*, was
registered previously and never used. Its current status is unknown. The
working brand for this and other work is **Joshua Jumbles**.

A second coupling: charging anything — paid app or IAP — requires accepting
Apple's Paid Applications agreement and supplying banking and tax details,
which is where the entity choice becomes concrete. A free app avoids this
entirely.

## Options

1. **Individual enrollment, no entity.** Zero setup; listing shows Joshua's
   legal name. Fine for a free app.
2. **Revive Jelly Bean Sciences.** Uses an existing registration, but the name
   diverges from the Joshua Jumbles brand.
3. **Form a Joshua Jumbles entity.** One brand across all work, cleanest story
   for a portfolio. Costs filing fees, a D-U-N-S number, and time.

## Open questions

- What is the current enrollment type on the Apple account?
- Is the Jelly Bean Sciences registration still live, dormant, or dissolved?
- Will v1 charge anything? If not, option 1 defers this cheaply.

## Notes
