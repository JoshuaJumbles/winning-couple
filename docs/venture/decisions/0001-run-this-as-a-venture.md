# 0001 — Run Winning Couple as a venture, not just an app

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-25

## Context

The app was started as practice and as a tangible portfolio piece. The core
loop is now complete and usable, which raises the question of what "done" means.
Stopping at a working build produces one kind of artifact; carrying it through
research, scope, beta, launch, and operation produces a different and more
credible one — especially for job-seeking, where "shipped and ran a product" is
a stronger claim than "wrote an app".

## Decision

Treat Winning Couple as a full independent product venture. The app is one
workstream among several: research, product, engineering, marketing,
legal/admin, operations, and the case study.

Two goals, in priority order:

1. **A real shipped product** — on the App Store, used by real couples.
2. **A portfolio artifact** — the decision trail, not just the binary.

These mostly align. Where they conflict, shipping wins; a polished case study
about an unshipped app is worth little.

## Consequences

- Work now spans domains where the "right" answer is a judgement call rather
  than a test result. Decisions get recorded (see 0002) so they can be defended
  later.
- Scope discipline matters more. The venture framing invites endless
  workstreams; the roadmap phases exist to keep them sequenced.
- Some work is deliberately lightweight — marketing and legal are exercises in
  covering the full spectrum competently, not in maximising outcomes.
- Monetisation is not assumed. Revenue may never materialise in this category,
  and that does not make the venture a failure.
